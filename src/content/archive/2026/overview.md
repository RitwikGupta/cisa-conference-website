The 2026 IEEE Conference on Computational Imaging Using Synthetic Apertures took place May 26–29 at the University at Albany in Albany, New York.

CISA connects computational imaging and remote sensing across theory, engineering practice, and standardization. The 2026 patron prospectus identified IEEE-USA, the IEEE Signal Processing Society, the IEEE Sensors Council, and the IEEE Synthetic Aperture Standards Committee among the participating organizations.
