CISA brings together researchers and engineers working on computational imaging and synthetic aperture methods across theory, engineering practice, and standardization.

The 2026 conference took place at the University at Albany in Albany, New York, May 26–29.

## Conference affiliations

The 2026 patron prospectus identified IEEE-USA, the IEEE Signal Processing Society, the IEEE Sensors Council, and the IEEE Synthetic Aperture Standards Committee among the participating organizations.
