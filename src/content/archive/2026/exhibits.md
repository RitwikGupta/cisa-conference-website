## Exhibiting at the Albany conference

The 2026 prospectus invited institutions to showcase imaging products and sensors, meet professionals from academia, government, and industry, introduce young professionals to their work, and build collaborations with researchers and entrepreneurs.

It described opportunities around keynote talks, invited special sessions, panels, and tutorials, and identified Albany, New York as the conference location.
