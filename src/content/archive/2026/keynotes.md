## Harbir Antil

**George Mason University**

Harbir Antil is Professor of Mathematics and founding director of the Center for Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence. His research connects optimization, partial differential equations, inverse problems, uncertainty quantification, and scientific computing with imaging, sensing, digital twins, and physics-informed AI.

## Davide Scaramuzza

**University of Zurich**

Davide Scaramuzza is Professor of Robotics and Perception. His work advances autonomous navigation and low-latency perception using standard and event-based cameras, including visual-inertial estimation and autonomous drones. He is an IEEE Fellow and co-author of Introduction to Autonomous Mobile Robots.

## Yu Sun

**Johns Hopkins University**

Yu Sun is an Assistant Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering and directs the Hopkins Computational Imaging Group. His research develops computational imaging algorithms and theory, integrating machine learning with challenging imaging problems. He earned his computer science Ph.D. at Washington University in St. Louis.

## Jinwei Ye

**George Mason University**

Jinwei Ye is an Associate Professor of Computer Science. Her research combines computational imaging, computer vision, and computer graphics to understand geometry and appearance. She received her Ph.D. from the University of Delaware and an NSF CAREER award in 2023.
