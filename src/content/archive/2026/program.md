The CISA 2026 program covered computational imaging and synthetic aperture methods through keynote talks, tutorials, and technical discussions. The full session timetable is not available.

## Keynote speakers

[Harbir Antil, Davide Scaramuzza, Yu Sun, and Jinwei Ye](../speakers/) brought perspectives from inverse problems, robotic perception, computational imaging, and computer vision.

## Tutorials

[Introduction to Synthetic Aperture Radar](../tutorials/) was presented by Armin Doerry of Sandia National Laboratories. Tutorials were included in the conference registration fee.

## Climate Change Initiative

The [Climate Change Initiative](../climate/) focused on sensors, measurements, and data interpretation for environmental monitoring, including opportunities to develop standards.
