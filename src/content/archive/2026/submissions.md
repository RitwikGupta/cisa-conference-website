## Submission system

[CISA 2026 on OpenReview](https://openreview.net/group?id=IEEE.org/CISA/2026) was the submission system for this edition. Its deadlines have passed.

## Paper requirements and dates

- Initial submissions: a minimum 2-page summary by March 24, 2026; complete 4+1-page papers were encouraged.
- Climate initiative summaries: March 24, 2026.
- Acceptance notification: April 6, 2026.
- Camera-ready papers: May 1, 2026; four pages plus one references-only page.
- Abstracts: 100–150 words.

## Camera-ready guidance

Authors uploaded their final version through their OpenReview author dashboard and identified it as camera-ready in the filename. The 2026 ISBN was **979-8-3195-2276-4**; manuscripts required the applicable IEEE, US government, Crown government, or European Union copyright notice.

IEEE PDF eXpress used conference ID **67324X**. Accepted authors completed IEEE's electronic copyright form, and camera-ready papers underwent plagiarism checks.

## Oral presentations

Slots allowed 17 minutes of presentation and 3 minutes of questions. Presenters brought a USB copy, embedded fonts, carried a backup PDF, and arrived 15 minutes early.

## Poster presentations

Portrait A0 posters measured 84.1 × 118.9 cm. Authors printed, installed, and removed their posters on assigned boards.

Live presentation was required for IEEE Xplore publication.
