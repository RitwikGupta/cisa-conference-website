## Technical scope

CISA invites research on synthetic-aperture and computational imaging, including physical models, data acquisition, and reconstruction algorithms. The call for papers covers synthetic-aperture radar and sonar, optical imaging, tomography, radio astronomy, medical imaging, and related inverse problems.

## Research from related fields

Contributions from related fields should address an imaging problem, explain the method, and identify its relevance to acquisition, reconstruction, or image interpretation. Relevant work includes vision-language models (VLMs), generative reconstruction, agentic imaging workflows, and cryo-electron microscopy and tomography (cryo-EM/ET).

## Author requirements

Submission deadlines, manuscript requirements, review details, and the submission portal will be announced.

The 2027 call for papers specifies an in-person event. Authors must present their papers live at the University of Maryland for publication in IEEE Xplore.
