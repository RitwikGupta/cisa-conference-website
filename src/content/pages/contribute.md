## A shared foundation. An open invitation.

The University of Maryland, IEEE-USA, and the IEEE Synthetic Aperture Standards Committee invite researchers and engineers to the fourth annual Conference on Computational Imaging Using Synthetic Apertures.

The 2027 call for papers connects remote sensing and computational imaging through physical models, data acquisition, and reconstruction algorithms. Its scope spans synthetic-aperture radar and sonar, optical imaging, tomography, radio astronomy, medical imaging, and related inverse problems.

## Where does your research fit?

We welcome connections to shared imaging problems: how to acquire informative measurements, reconstruct from incomplete data, and understand the resulting images. Researchers working on VLMs, generative models, agentic imaging workflows, and cryo-EM/ET are invited to explore these connections with CISA.

You do not need to work in radar to find common ground here. Explain the imaging problem, the role of your method, and what researchers in another field could learn from it.

## Preparing to contribute

The official PDF describes the conference’s technical scope. Submission deadlines, manuscript requirements, review details, and the 2027 submission portal will be published here when confirmed.

The 2027 call for papers specifies an in-person event. Authors must present their papers live at the University of Maryland for publication in IEEE Xplore.
