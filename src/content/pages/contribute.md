## Technical scope

CISA invites research that combines physical models, data acquisition, and computational algorithms to improve image reconstruction beyond conventional sensor capabilities. The conference connects remote sensing applications with computational imaging across disciplines, covering advances in theory, engineering practice, and standardization.

Contributions may address forward imaging models, sensor and hardware systems, sampling strategies, or reconstruction in the presence of measurement noise, system nonlinearities, and limited or irregular sampling. Synthetic-aperture methods and related approaches that connect measurements to k-space samples are within scope.

## Research topics

The call for papers includes the following topics:

- **SAR:** automotive, mmWave, and THz SAR; polarimetric, interferometric, and tomographic SAR; ISAR; 3-D imaging; image reconstruction methods; novel front-end sensors.
- **Radar:** weather radar and phased array radar.
- **Sonar:** micro-navigation, bathymetry, and wideband regimes.
- **Optics:** Fourier ptychography and synthetic-aperture microscopy; synthetic-aperture holography; coded diffraction and coded aperture imaging; single-photon cameras; LED or VCSEL array imaging.
- **Seismology:** wave migration and localization techniques.
- **K-space modeling and reconstruction techniques:** black-hole imaging, radio astronomy, and low magnetic field MRI.
- **Scientific imaging:** inverse problems with physical meaning.
- **Tomography:** X-ray and Doppler techniques.
- **Synthetic-aperture radiometry:** Earth observation, environmental monitoring, and planetary imaging.
- **Super-resolution:** methods that exploit the physics of sensing.
- **Time-of-flight imaging:** synthetic apertures via multi-aperture and multi-camera setups; 3-D imaging; optical and sub-optical wavelengths.
- **GNSS reflectometry:** soil moisture, vegetation, and sea ice.
- **Model-based deep reconstruction:** plug-and-play methods, diffusion models for image priors, physics-informed neural networks, and Doppler radiance fields.
- **Point cloud processing:** synthetic-aperture LiDAR (SAL) and 4-D mmWave SAR.
- **Magnetic field cameras:** industrial and medical applications.
- **Compressed sensing:** sub-Nyquist data acquisition and sparsity.
- **Climate:** remote sensing of Earth's natural environment.

## Research from related fields

Contributions from related fields should explain their connection to a physical imaging problem: how the method uses imaging physics or measurement structure, or improves acquisition, reconstruction, or analysis of the resulting data.

Relevant connections include vision-language models (VLMs) for reasoning about imaging measurements and reconstructions; agentic methods for experiment design, acquisition, and reconstruction workflows; and cryo-electron microscopy and tomography (cryo-EM/ET) as physical inverse problems in structural imaging. Generative methods can contribute learned priors within model-based reconstruction.

These examples describe research connections, not confirmed sessions or tutorials.

## Special sessions and tutorials

CISA invites proposals for special sessions and tutorials within the conference's technical scope. The proposal deadline is listed under Important dates. Proposal instructions and the submission destination will be announced.

## Author requirements

The call specifies **4+1-page** initial submissions and **4+1-page** camera-ready papers. Manuscript templates, detailed formatting and review instructions, and the submission portal will be announced.

CISA 2027 is an in-person event. Authors must present their papers live at the University of Maryland for publication in IEEE Xplore.
