## Why exhibit at CISA?

CISA brings together computational imaging researchers and engineers from academia, government, and industry. Its scope spans theory, engineering practice, and standardization.

- Showcase your products, sensors, and services to imaging professionals.
- Meet researchers and technical leaders who evaluate equipment and services for their institutions.
- Introduce your institution to young professionals entering computational imaging.
- Build collaborations with researchers and entrepreneurs.
- Support technical exchange through keynote talks, special sessions, panel discussions, and tutorials.

CISA 2027 takes place June 1–3 at the Iribe Center, University of Maryland, in College Park, Maryland. The technical program and speakers will be announced.
