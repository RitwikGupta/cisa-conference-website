## Conference purpose

The IEEE Conference on Computational Imaging Using Synthetic Apertures (CISA) connects remote sensing with computational imaging across disciplines. Its focus is the combination of data acquisition, forward imaging models, and reconstruction algorithms to improve what sensing systems can recover.

The conference covers advances in theory, engineering practice, and standardization. Topics include measurement design, sensor and hardware systems, physical inverse problems, and reconstruction under noise, system nonlinearities, or limited and irregular sampling.

## History

CISA 2027 is the fourth annual conference. It will be hosted at the University of Maryland in College Park.
