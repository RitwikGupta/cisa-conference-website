## Connected by the questions we ask

What can we learn from incomplete measurements? How can a physical model improve a reconstruction? What becomes possible when sensing and computation are designed together?

These questions connect synthetic apertures to a much wider imaging community. CISA creates space for researchers to exchange methods, examine new applications, and discover useful connections across fields.

## Building a continuing conversation

The 2027 call for papers identifies CISA as the fourth annual conference, with a focus on advances in theory, engineering practice, and standardization. The next gathering brings that conversation to the University of Maryland.

Verified links to earlier programs and proceedings will be added as the conference history is assembled.
