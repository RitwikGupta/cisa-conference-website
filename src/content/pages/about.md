## Conference purpose

The IEEE Conference on Computational Imaging Using Synthetic Apertures (CISA) covers the theory, methods, and applications of synthetic-aperture and computational imaging. It addresses physical models, measurement systems, reconstruction algorithms, and standardization across imaging disciplines.

## History

CISA 2027 is the fourth annual conference. It will be hosted at the University of Maryland in College Park.
