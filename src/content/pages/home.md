title: 2027 IEEE Conference on Computational Imaging Using Synthetic Apertures
description: CISA 2027, June 1–3 at the University of Maryland. Physics-based computational imaging, synthetic apertures, engineering practice, and standardization.
intro: 'CISA focuses on physics-based computational imaging: combining data acquisition, forward models, and reconstruction algorithms to recover information beyond conventional sensor capabilities. It connects synthetic-aperture methods in remote sensing with related approaches across imaging, covering advances in theory, engineering practice, and standardization.'
---

CISA connects researchers and engineers working on the physical and computational foundations of imaging. The 2027 conference will be hosted at the University of Maryland.
